ZMODEM for POSIX systems

Small tools.

zmtx and zmrx are compact command-line programs for sending and receiving files with the ZMODEM protocol.

What it is

A focused ZMODEM implementation

01

Portable

Strict C99 and POSIX.1-2001 source, tested with modern GCC and Clang on Linux, macOS and FreeBSD.

02

Compatible

Binary transfers, CRC16 and CRC32, crash recovery, streaming and non-streaming operation.

03

Fast

Buffered escaping, batched CRC32, sliding transmit windows and optional 4 KiB or 8 KiB ZedZap blocks.

The source is available under the 2-clause BSD license. Historical third-party notices remain intact.

Where it came from

More than thirty years of history

  1. July 1994

    The original release

    Jacques Mattheij of Mattheij Computer Service publishes zmtx/zmrx as a fresh, compact ZMODEM implementation and programmer-oriented protocol reference.

  2. 2003 onward

    A life inside Synchronet

    Synchronet develops a descendant of this code over many years. Its long history later provides a useful record of protocol fixes that can be brought back to the standalone package.

  3. 19 November 2015

    Copyright transferred

    Jacques Mattheij transfers to Stephen Hurd his copyright and all other rights he held in the released zmodem package and its associated files, naming Fernando Toledo and Rob Swindell as witnesses. The transfer is documented in Synchronet's source history.

  4. August 2026

    The standalone project returns

    The original sources are preserved in Git, relicensed under BSD-2-Clause, modernized for current systems, and reunited with applicable fixes from their Synchronet descendant. Version 2.00 adds ZedZap, non-streaming transfers, sliding windows, performance improvements and extensive protocol tests.

  5. 25 August 2026

    The sexyz benchmark battle

    Receive and transmit paths learn to process ordinary unescaped bytes and CRCs in bulk as zmtx/zmrx goes head-to-head with Synchronet's sexyz. The contest ends in a draw when the test setup reaches its throughput ceiling before either implementation does.
